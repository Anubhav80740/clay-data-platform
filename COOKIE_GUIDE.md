# 🍪 Quick Teammate Guide: Connecting Your Clay Account

Follow these quick 1-minute steps to connect your Clay account to the extraction platform.

---

### 📍 Step 1: Sign Up / Sign In
1. Open the web platform link.
2. If it is your first time, click the **'✨ Sign Up / Register'** tab, pick a username & password, and sign in.

---

### 📍 Step 2: Grab Your Cookie from Clay (30 Seconds)
1. Open your regular browser (Chrome, Edge, or Brave) where you are **already logged into Clay** (https://app.clay.com).
2. Open Developer Tools:
   - **Windows**: Press **F12** (or right-click anywhere -> **Inspect**).
   - **Mac**: Press **Cmd + Option + I**.
3. In Developer Tools:
   - Click the **Network** tab at the top.
   - Refresh the page (**F5**).
   - In the filter box, search for: my-workspaces or pi.clay.com.
4. Right-click on the request in the list -> **Copy** -> **Copy as cURL (bash)** (or copy the Cookie header from Request Headers).

---

### 📍 Step 3: Paste & Save in the Platform
1. Go back to our **Clay Data Platform** tab.
2. Look at the **top navigation bar** (top right, next to your username badge).
3. Click the **'📝 Paste'** button.
4. Paste what you copied into the box and click **'Save & Verify Cookie'**.
5. Your badge will instantly turn **'🟢 Cookie: Active'**!

---

### 📍 Step 4: Ready to Extract!
- You do **not** need to do this again for future downloads.
- Your session is now saved to your profile and isolated from other teammates.
- You can immediately run **Step 1 (Count)**, **Step 2 (Plan)**, and **Step 3 (Download)**!
